## Help Support Open Engineering Scholarship on engrXiv

engrXiv is seeking volunteer moderators to help maintain the quality, relevance, and integrity of submissions to our preprint server. Moderators play an important role in ensuring that manuscripts align with engrXiv's mission, scope, and submission guidelines while supporting a welcoming and accessible platform for the engineering research community.

This is a volunteer service position and provides an opportunity to contribute to open scholarly communication, engage with emerging research across engineering disciplines, and support researchers worldwide.

## Role Description

Volunteer moderators review newly submitted preprints before they are posted publicly on the server. Reviews focus on compliance with engrXiv policies and moderation guidelines rather than on scientific merit or peer review.

Moderators are expected to:

- Review submitted manuscripts against engrXiv's moderation and content policies.
- Verify that submissions fall within the scope of engineering and related fields supported by the server.
- Check for compliance with basic submission requirements and community standards.
- Identify submissions that may require further review or escalation.
- Communicate respectfully and professionally with fellow moderators and administrators when questions arise.

## Minimum Qualifications

Applicants should possess:

- A bachelor's degree in engineering or a closely related field, or equivalent professional experience.
- Familiarity with scholarly publishing, preprints, or academic research.
- Ability to evaluate whether a manuscript aligns with stated submission guidelines.
- Strong attention to detail and good judgment.
- Commitment to fair, consistent, and unbiased review practices.
- Ability to communicate professionally in written English.

## Preferred Qualifications

Preference may be given to applicants with:

- A graduate degree in engineering or a related discipline.
- Experience as a researcher, author, reviewer, editor, or conference organizer.
- Prior involvement with open-access publishing, repositories, or preprint servers.
- Knowledge of research integrity, publication ethics, or scholarly communication practices.
- Experience working in multidisciplinary engineering fields.
- Interest in supporting open science and open engineering initiatives.
- Weekly availability.

## Time Commitment

Moderation workload varies throughout the year. Most moderators review submissions on a flexible schedule and contribute as their availability permits. Preference for the ability to provide some volunteer time each week.

## How to Volunteer

Individuals interested in serving as a volunteer moderator are encouraged to submit a brief statement of interest describing their background, areas of expertise, and relevant experience, along with their CV by email to `volunteer [at] engrxiv.org`.

Join us in supporting open, accessible, and community-driven engineering scholarship through engrXiv.
